About

Arrow Escape: Mochi's Quest v3.0

Made by Sparkle Forge Studios. 500 levels across 10 worlds, every one solvable.

How to play

🏹 Tap an arrow to shoot it off the board.
🚫 If another arrow blocks its path, you lose a life.
❤ You have 5 lives per level. Keep them all for 3 stars.
🔥 Chain clean shots for combo points.
🪙 Earn coins to buy hints, lives and outfits for Mochi.
🐾 Tap Mochi to pet her!
